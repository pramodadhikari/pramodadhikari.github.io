Climate assessments & reports

CO-AUTHOR / CLIMATE ASSESSMENT

Snake River Headwaters Futures

I contributed as a co-author to the development of Snake River Headwaters Futures, studying historical climate and future climate conditions to support the assessment of climate change in the headwaters region.

CO-LEAD, CLIMATE DATA ANALYSIS / 2026

Colorado Climate Roadmap report

I co-led the climate data analysis for the 2026 Colorado Climate Roadmap report, examining the impacts of warming at different global warming levels (GWLs). The analysis draws on an ensemble of 25 high-resolution regional Earth system model simulations at 9 km resolution, together with RM1.3 simulations at 1.33 km resolution.
