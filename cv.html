Summary
I specialize in high-resolution regional Earth system modeling, focusing on the regional impacts of a warming climate on hydrometeorology.

Key Skills

Regional Climate Modeling
WRF & WRF-Chem
Advanced expertise in configuring, modifying, and executing WRF and WRF-Chem simulations for regional climate and air quality applications at fine spatial and temporal scales.
Climate Data Analysis
Observational, Reanalysis, CMIP6, LENS2
Extensive experience analyzing regional and global climate datasets, with a focus on hydroclimate extremes and climate change impacts across mountainous terrains.
Gridded Data Processing
xarray, numpy, scipy, pandas, matplotlib
Skilled in handling and visualizing large-scale spatial and temporal datasets using Python-based tools to generate diagnostics and publication-quality figures.
High-Performance Computing

Extensive experience with supercomputing environments, including parallel processing, PBS job scripting, and data workflow optimization on NCAR systems.
Scientific Programming
Python, R, FORTRAN
Strong programming background for data analysis, modeling, and workflow automation. Daily user of Jupyter Notebooks and well-versed in modular, reproducible code design.
Linux/Unix Environments
Shell Scripting & System Tools
